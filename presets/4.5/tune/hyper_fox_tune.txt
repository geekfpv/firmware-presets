#$ DESCRIPTION: ## Before you fly
#$ DESCRIPTION: - You need RPM filters for this tune, if your hardware is incompatible update it.
#$ DESCRIPTION: - F7 flight controller: DShot600 and pid loop 8 kHz. F4 flight controller: DShot300 and pid loop 4 kHz. 
#$ DESCRIPTION: - Better start uning this preset on a clean flash. Configure ports, modes, rates, and VTX table and go.
#$ DESCRIPTION: - Check motor ordering and motors rotation **WITHOUT PROPELLERS ON**.
#$ DESCRIPTION: - Do yur first test flight in hovering/LOS, you may hear noise in hovering, check motors temperature, if they are super hot, check your mechanics.
#$ DESCRIPTION: - Try flying your drone without pushing too hard, check again motors. If they are not hot, you can go.
